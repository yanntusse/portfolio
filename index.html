How I work

Principles behind
every engagement

01
Diagnose before building
I map how work actually moves before recommending anything. Most problems look like execution issues but are really coordination or clarity issues upstream.
02
Systems over heroics
The goal is never to be indispensable. I build systems predictable enough to run without daily intervention so your team scales without adding overhead.
03
Simple beats sophisticated
A workflow your team actually uses beats an elegant one they ignore. I design for adoption first and optimize based on what is genuinely working.
04
Visibility creates clarity
Leaders make better decisions when they see the full picture in one place. I build reporting that surfaces what matters without adding noise.
